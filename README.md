# Caspri E-commerce Bloom

Build a complete, modern, premium e-commerce website for:

BUSINESS NAME:

Caspri Natural Spring Water Comp. LTD

TAGLINE:

“Bottled by Nature, Perfected by Innovation”

BUSINESS:

Caspri Natural Spring Water Comp. LTD is a natural spring water bottling company focused on bottling and selling high-quality natural spring water.

PRIMARY PRODUCT:

Bottled Natural Spring Water

WEBSITE GOAL:

Create a premium, trustworthy, conversion-focused e-commerce website where customers can learn about Caspri Natural Spring Water, explore available water products, select bottle sizes/quantities, add products to cart, and complete an online purchase.

OVERALL DESIGN DIRECTION:

Create a visually stunning premium bottled-water brand website inspired by high-end international water brands. The design should communicate:

- Purity

- Nature

- Freshness

- Premium quality

- Trust

- Health-conscious living

- Sustainability

- Modern innovation

Use a clean, sophisticated visual style with lots of white space, beautiful natural-water imagery, elegant typography, subtle animations, glass/water effects, flowing curves, and premium product photography.

COLOR PALETTE:

- Clean white

- Crystal/light blue

- Deep ocean blue

- Aqua/turquoise accents

- Very subtle natural green

- Dark navy for premium contrast

Avoid making the site look like a generic grocery store. It should feel like a premium bottled-water brand.

NAVIGATION:

Create a sticky responsive navigation bar with:

Logo: Caspri Natural Spring Water Comp. LTD

Menu:

- Home

- Our Water

- Shop

- About Us

- Sustainability

- Contact

Right side:

- Search icon

- Account icon

- Shopping cart icon with live item count

- Primary “Shop Water” CTA

MOBILE:

Create a polished mobile navigation with hamburger menu, accessible buttons, and an optimized shopping experience.
